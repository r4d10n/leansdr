   - Second-order PI PLL (proportional-integral)

   - freq_beta = 0.0012/omega (slow integral path)
   - Constellation-aware frequency bounds to prevent phase ambiguity
   - Fixed-point: phase ∈ [0, 65536), freqw in Hz*65536

5. CLOCK RECOVERY
   - Three sampler interfaces: nearest, linear, FIR
   - FIR sampler: 21-511 taps, dynamically derotated for carrier
   - Fractional delay: mu ∈ [0,1) between symbol samples
   - Frequency update throttled: every 16*ncoeffs symbols (~10% overhead)

6. COMPUTATIONAL COMPLEXITY
   Main bottlenecks identified:
   a) FIR interpolation: 44 cycles/symbol (21 taps)
      - 20-30% of total demodulator time

   b) TED calculation: 6-8 cycles/symbol
      - 5-10% of total time

   c) AGC power loops: 3-4 cycles per sample
      - 5-10% of total time
      - NEON target: 4x speedup
      
   d) Constellation lookup: <1% (cache-dominated)
      - Pre-computed, already optimized
      - No parallelization needed

7. MEMORY FOOTPRINT

   - Rotator LUT (sin/cos): 512 KB
   - Fast QPSK LUTs: 1+ MB
   - Total: ~2 MB for lookup tables
   - Cache efficiency: Critical for FIR performance

8. DATA FLOW PATTERNS
   - Chunk-based processing (128 samples/chunk)
   - Output: ~90-110 symbols per chunk
   - Measurement decimation: Every ~8K chunks
   - Branching paths: Three output types (freq, SS, MER)
